# V2 场景生成 Prompt 记录

执行模式：Codex 内置 image generation。所有场景均使用原始正面、背面、平铺或多色图作为产品结构参考。

## 1. 城市主视觉

```text
Photorealistic Premium A+ hero banner. Adult woman walking through an elegant contemporary city colonnade while wearing the exact black long vertically ribbed blazer-cardigan from the references. Preserve blue denim-look lapels, full placket, exactly four buttons, two blue-trim front pockets, blue turn-up cuffs and blue hem. Subject on the right, clean negative space on the left. No logo, text or watermark.
```

定点修正：仅将门襟修正为一侧一列恰好四粒纽扣、另一侧四个扣眼；人物、姿态、背景、光线与其他服装结构保持不变。

## 2. 正背双姿态

```text
Premium fashion studio composite with the same adult woman wearing the exact referenced product in two poses: clear front three-quarter view on the left and clear back three-quarter view on the right. Preserve the black ribbed long silhouette and every blue trim location. The back remains plain black ribbed. No invented belt, zipper, hood, seams, logo, text or watermark.
```

## 3. 袖口动作近景

```text
Premium boutique fitting-area scene. Adult woman wears the exact garment and naturally adjusts one blue turn-up cuff. Keep the lapels, placket, visible pockets, long hem and black ribbed surface readable. Change only environment and pose; no garment redesign, logo, text or watermark.
```

## 4. 咖啡街区日常场景

```text
Photorealistic candid European café-district scene. Adult woman walks naturally while wearing the exact black-and-blue long ribbed blazer-cardigan, carrying a small neutral tote. Preserve all trim locations, four buttons, two front pockets and long straight cut. No business names, logos, text or watermark.
```

## 5. 画廊背面场景

```text
Premium back-view lifestyle banner in a bright contemporary art gallery. Adult woman walks away naturally, showing the true plain black ribbed back, long straight hem, blue collar edge and both blue turn-up cuffs. Do not invent back vents, belts, seams, pockets, logos, text or watermark.
```

## 6. 五色真人并排

```text
Premium studio lineup of exactly five adult women standing side by side, each wearing the same exact long ribbed blazer-cardigan in one supported color: black, warm brown, cream, deep red and navy. Each keeps identical blue denim-look lapels, placket, cuffs, pocket trim and hem. Clean horizontal lineup, equal visual weight, no copied collage tiles, no overlap, no logos, text or watermark.
```

## 7. 情侣三场景斜线画板

```text
One photorealistic wide banner showing the same adult couple in three lifestyle moments: European city arcade, café exterior and urban park. Separate the scenes with two clean diagonal near-vertical dividers. The woman wears the exact referenced black-and-blue long cardigan in every scene; the man wears a coordinated neutral jacket. Preserve consistent faces, clothing and product construction. No text, logos or watermark.
```

## 8. 运动氛围场景

```text
Sporty urban lifestyle banner at an outdoor tennis and fitness club. The woman walks briskly down broad steps carrying a neutral gym tote and reusable bottle; she is not exercising in the jacket. Preserve the exact black ribbed long cut, blue trim, four buttons, two pockets and blue hem. No performance claims, logos, text or watermark.
```

## 9. 参考版式多色画板

```text
Use the supplied Jessika image as layout-density reference only. Build a new board with five equal vertical outdoor portrait panels at the top, an empty warm-ivory German-title band in the middle, and five isolated flat-product views at the bottom. Color order: black, warm brown, deep red, cream and soft pink. Identical garment construction in all ten views. No navy, text, logos or watermark.
```
